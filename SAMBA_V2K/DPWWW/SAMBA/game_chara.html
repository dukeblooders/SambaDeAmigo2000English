Character introductions
Introducing some fun samba friends!

[Amigo]

A monkey who loves maracas. He's the main character of this game and is always in a good mood!
[Amiga]

Speaking of new characters, here's Amigo's younger sister, Amiga! Isn't she cute?
[Linda]

A dancer and a true idol in town. Amigo also seems to have fallen in love with her.
[Bingo & Bongo]

They think no one knows their true identities behind these disguises, but everyone actually knows.
[Rio]

Despite his feminine appearance, Rio is actually a man... Did you notice?
[Chumba & Wamba]

Chumba is a former gang leader who likes to play the mandolin, while Wamba, a former bartender, plays the congas.
[Pose]

Who is this shadowy mastermind shrouded in mystery? He always speaks in an authoritative tone, saying things like "Dance!" or "Strike a pose!". He also seems to be quite good at volleyball.
