Lesson 8
Precautions for the Dreamcast maracas

1. Do not shake maracas close to the ground.
The sensor cannot read accurately if the maracas are only 60 cm above the ground. When you shake them downward, be careful not to go too low!
2. Spacing with maracas
If the maracas are held too far apart, they may hit people or objects, which can be dangerous. If they're too close together, the tracking may become inaccurate. Find the right balance!
3. Reaction areas of the maracas
The maracas' reaction areas are actually concentric circles (red at the top, yellow in the middle, and green at the bottom), as shown in the image. Be careful not to move too far from the footprints on the mat, or your height reading may change!
4. Orientation of maracas
According to a certain Sonic Team member, holding the maracas with the sensor facing forward, as shown in the image, is the best way to use them!
5. Check if the cable is unplugged
Sometimes, especially after a long day of gaming, the cable may start to come loose from the base unit. Check the cable if your maracas are no longer responding!
