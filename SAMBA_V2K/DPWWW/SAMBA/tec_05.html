Lesson 5
Let's play in rhythm

Feel the music with your body!
A common issue among beginners is that they focus only on the balls, but they don't appear just by chance. It's important to play in rhythm. To begin, let's follow the rhythm by lightly tapping our feet! Something will definitely happen!

Songs like "TAKE ON ME" can be confusing because the rhythm is off-beat. But, it feels great once you get it right, so do your best to memorize it!
