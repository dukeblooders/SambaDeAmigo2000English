Lesson 7
Mini-game strategy

1. Master your receiving skill!
Do you know how to receive? You'll get the ball if you wait, but shaking your maracas at the right moment will send the ball higher, making it easier to follow up with your next attack. Once you master receiving, you'll even be able to counter your opponent's power attacks!
2. Use power attacks!
The key to a power attack is deciding which hand will receive the ball and which one will smash. Then hit the ball repeatedly! Once you get used to it, you should be able to smash with full power!
3. A foolproof block!
When your opponent attacks, you can block the shot by staying in the air just above the net and shaking your maracas at the right moment!
4. Feint!
If your opponent is preparing to block, try a feint by lobbing instead of shooting. It's especially effective, and annoying, when playing against another person!
5. More advanced techniques
There are many more techniques to discover, especially when playing against a friend in VS mode. Try developing your own original techniques, such as a sudden double attack, feint a shot, etc.
